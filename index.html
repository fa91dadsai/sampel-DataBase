04. Sommelier Secrets

💡 The Upsell Strategy
If they order Pasta with Red Sauce, sell Standard Chianti (high acid loves tomato).
If they order Steak or Truffles, sell Classico (tannins crave protein).
🌡️ Temperature
Standard: 14–16°C (light chill).
Classico: 16–18°C (cellar cool).
Never serve warm: heat emphasizes alcohol over fruit.
🍷 Glass & Service
Decanting: Standard (No). Classico Riserva (Yes, 30 mins).
Glassware: Use a broad bowl (Bordeaux) for Classico to open the earthy notes.
